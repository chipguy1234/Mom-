Test-day rules Answer every multiple-choice question (no penalty for guessing). Budget ~10 minutes per written response and don't let them eat your multiple-choice time. On every question, ask: "What would the ideal, by-the-book New York teacher do?" — not what you'd personally do. The most collaborative, proactive, asset-based answer that keeps the student in the general-education classroom is almost always the credited one.
